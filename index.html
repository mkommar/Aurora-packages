The premise

Make the boundary the product.

Most operating systems are impressive because of their scale. Aurora is interesting because of its shape: a deliberately narrow x86-64 system where process address spaces, IPC checks, paging, scheduling, filesystems, and the compatibility ABI can be studied as one connected artifact.

It is not trying to win a feature checklist. It is trying to make the difficult parts legible.
